Introduction

If you are new to SAP, you may hear terms such as SAP, ERP, SAP SuccessFactors, HCM and Employee Central and wonder how they are connected.

SAP is widely used by organizations to manage important business processes. SAP SuccessFactors is part of the broader SAP ecosystem and focuses on cloud-based Human Experience Management and HR processes.

This article explains the basics in a simple way and provides a starting point for anyone beginning their SAP SuccessFactors learning journey.

What Is SAP?

SAP is a global enterprise software company known for developing software that helps organizations manage their business processes and business data.

Companies use SAP solutions to support different areas of their business, including finance, procurement, supply chain, sales and human resources.

Simple Definition

SAP provides enterprise software that helps organizations manage and connect important business processes.

Instead of different departments working with completely separate systems, enterprise software can help organizations manage connected business processes and data.

What Is ERP?

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning.

An ERP system is designed to help organizations manage and connect important business activities through a centralized system.

For example, different business functions may need information from finance, procurement, inventory, employees or other business areas.

Enterprise software helps organizations manage these processes in a more integrated way.

Why Do Companies Use SAP?

Large and growing organizations often have complex business processes and significant amounts of data.

Managing these processes through disconnected tools, spreadsheets and individual applications can become difficult.

SAP solutions can help organizations standardize processes, manage business data and support operations across different business functions.

Common business areas supported by SAP

  • Finance and accounting
  • Procurement
  • Supply chain processes
  • Sales and customer processes
  • Human resources and workforce management

What Is SAP SuccessFactors?

SAP SuccessFactors is SAP's cloud-based solution for managing many HR and workforce-related processes.

It helps organizations manage employee information, HR transactions, recruiting, learning, performance, compensation and other people-related processes.

Simple Definition

SAP SuccessFactors is a cloud-based HR solution that helps organizations manage employees and workforce-related processes.

Because it is cloud-based, organizations access the system through the internet rather than relying only on traditional on-premise software installations.

Main SAP SuccessFactors Modules

SAP SuccessFactors includes multiple modules. An organization may use some or many of these depending on its HR requirements.

Employee Central

Employee Central is the core HR module used to manage important employee and employment information.

Recruiting

Recruiting supports recruitment-related processes, such as managing job requisitions and candidates.

Onboarding

Onboarding supports activities involved when new employees join an organization.

Learning

Learning supports employee learning, training and development activities.

Performance and Goals

These modules support goal management, performance processes and employee development discussions.

Compensation

Compensation supports compensation planning and related reward processes.

These modules can work together to support the employee lifecycle.

What Is Employee Central?

Employee Central is one of the core modules within SAP SuccessFactors and is commonly used to manage employee and employment-related information.

It supports important HR processes throughout an employee's lifecycle.

Examples include:

  • Hiring an employee
  • Managing personal information
  • Managing job and employment information
  • Processing promotions and transfers
  • Managing organizational changes
  • Recording future-dated employee changes
  • Managing termination processes

Employee Central is therefore an important starting point for anyone interested in SAP SuccessFactors Core HR consulting.

Next Learning Step

If you want to focus on SAP SuccessFactors consulting, Employee Central is one of the best areas to understand first because many other HR processes depend on employee and organizational data.

SAP vs SAP SuccessFactors

SAP is the broader enterprise software ecosystem, while SAP SuccessFactors focuses primarily on cloud-based HR and workforce processes.

A simple way to understand the relationship is:

SAP → Enterprise software ecosystem
SAP SuccessFactors → Cloud HR solutions within the SAP ecosystem
Employee Central → Core HR module within SAP SuccessFactors

Why Cloud-Based HR Systems Matter

HR teams manage large amounts of employee information and support many processes throughout the employee lifecycle.

Cloud-based HR systems can help organizations access centralized information, support standardized processes and manage HR activities across different locations.

Organizations may also receive system enhancements and updates through the cloud software model.

SAP SuccessFactors Career Opportunities

SAP SuccessFactors provides multiple career paths for professionals interested in HR technology and enterprise software.

Depending on your experience and interests, you may work in areas such as:

  • Employee Central consulting
  • Recruiting consulting
  • Learning consulting
  • Performance and Goals
  • Compensation
  • Integration and SAP Cloud Integration
  • Data migration and reporting
  • Application support and AMS

Consultants often develop expertise in one core area first and then expand their knowledge into related modules or technologies.

Where Should Beginners Start?

If you are completely new to SAP SuccessFactors, it is helpful to build your knowledge step by step.

Step 1: Understand SAP basics

Learn what SAP is and why enterprise software is used by organizations.

Step 2: Understand SAP SuccessFactors

Learn the purpose of SAP SuccessFactors and become familiar with its main modules.

Step 3: Learn Employee Central fundamentals

Understand employee data, organizational structures, effective dating, foundation objects and other core concepts.

Step 4: Learn configuration concepts

Explore how consultants configure and manage Employee Central functionality.

Step 5: Learn real-world scenarios

Practical scenarios help connect configuration concepts with real business requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SAP the same as SAP SuccessFactors?

No. SAP is the broader enterprise software ecosystem. SAP SuccessFactors is a cloud-based HR solution within the broader SAP product portfolio.

What is SAP SuccessFactors mainly used for?

SAP SuccessFactors is used to support HR and workforce-related processes such as employee data, recruiting, learning, performance and compensation.

What is Employee Central?

Employee Central is a core SAP SuccessFactors module used to manage employee and employment-related information and HR processes.

Is SAP SuccessFactors a good career option?

SAP SuccessFactors can provide career opportunities for professionals interested in HR technology, enterprise applications, consulting, implementation and application support.

Can a beginner learn SAP SuccessFactors?

Yes. Beginners can start by understanding SAP, SAP SuccessFactors and Employee Central fundamentals before progressing into configuration and practical scenarios.

Conclusion

SAP helps organizations manage important business processes, while SAP SuccessFactors focuses on cloud-based HR and workforce processes.

For beginners interested in SAP SuccessFactors, understanding the relationship between SAP, SuccessFactors and Employee Central provides an important foundation.
