Concurrent Employment in SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central

Understand what Concurrent Employment is, when it is used, how it works in Employee Central and the important concepts consultants should know.

In SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central, employees may sometimes have more than one employment relationship with an organization. This requirement is handled through the concept of Concurrent Employment.

What is Concurrent Employment?

Concurrent Employment allows an individual to have multiple employment relationships at the same time within the organization.

Instead of treating every relationship as a completely different person, Employee Central can maintain the employment relationships while keeping the individual's identity connected.

Simple Definition

Concurrent Employment means that the same individual can have multiple active employment relationships with the organization at the same time.

Why Do Organizations Use Concurrent Employment?

The requirement usually comes from a business situation where one person needs to be represented through more than one employment relationship.

For example, an organization may need to maintain separate employment information for different roles, assignments, organizational units or other business requirements.

Typical Business Considerations

  • The individual has more than one employment relationship.
  • Each relationship may require different employment information.
  • The organization needs to distinguish between the different relationships.
  • Downstream systems may need information about the different employment relationships.

How Concurrent Employment Works

The most important concept to understand is the difference between the person and the employment relationship.

A person represents the individual, while an employment relationship represents that person's relationship with the organization.

When concurrent employment is required, the consultant should understand which information belongs to the person and which information belongs to each employment.

Real-World Example

Let's consider a simple example.

An employee is working for an organization in two different employment capacities. The business wants to maintain separate employment information for each relationship while still recognizing that both relationships belong to the same individual.

In this type of scenario, the consultant needs to understand how the business wants employee information to be maintained, reported and integrated.

Consultant Tip

Don't start with configuration. Start with the business requirement. First determine why the organization needs multiple employment relationships and what information must be different between them.

Important Points for SAP Consultants

When you receive a Concurrent Employment requirement, there are several questions you should clarify with the business before designing the solution.

1. What is the Business Requirement?

Understand why the organization needs multiple employment relationships. Avoid assuming that every multiple-role scenario requires concurrent employment.

2. What Information Should Be Different?

Identify which employment-related information needs to be maintained separately for each relationship.

3. What About Integrations?

Always consider downstream systems. Employee Central data may be consumed by payroll, time management, finance, identity management or other applications.

4. What About Reporting?

Discuss how HR teams expect the employee and employment relationships to appear in reporting and analytics.

5. Test the Complete Lifecycle

Testing should not stop after creating the employment relationship. Consider changes, corrections, termination scenarios and downstream integrations as part of the overall solution.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Treating every multiple-role requirement as Concurrent Employment.
  • Starting configuration before understanding the business process.
  • Ignoring downstream integration requirements.
  • Not considering reporting requirements.
  • Testing only the initial creation scenario.

Concurrent Employment Interview Questions

What is Concurrent Employment?

Concurrent Employment refers to a scenario where the same individual has multiple employment relationships with the organization at the same time.

When would you consider Concurrent Employment?

It should be considered when the business requires multiple employment relationships to be maintained separately for the same individual.

What should a consultant understand before configuring it?

The consultant should understand the business requirement, employment relationships, data ownership, reporting, integrations and the expected employee lifecycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Concurrent Employment the same as having multiple roles?

Not necessarily. Having multiple roles does not automatically mean that multiple employment relationships are required. The business requirement should determine the appropriate solution.

Why are integrations important?

Because employee data maintained in Employee Central may be consumed by other systems. A solution should therefore consider how each employment relationship is represented downstream.

What is the most important thing for a consultant?

Understand the business requirement first. Configuration should come after the business process and data requirements are clearly understood.

Conclusion

Concurrent Employment is an important concept in SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central for organizations that need to maintain multiple employment relationships for the same individual.

From a consultant's perspective, the most important part is not simply knowing the terminology. You should understand the business scenario, determine what information needs to be maintained separately, and consider reporting and downstream integrations before designing the solution.

This business-first approach helps ensure that the Employee Central solution supports the organization's actual requirements rather than simply implementing configuration without understanding the underlying process.
